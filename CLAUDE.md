## Project knowledge base

`wiki/` is an agent-maintained knowledge base about this project: how features work, the
business rules behind them and how they are implemented. For questions about the
behavior, rules or implementation of a feature, consult the wiki first and follow
[wiki/SCHEMA.md](wiki/SCHEMA.md). Do not edit wiki pages by hand; use the wiki skills
(`wiki-query`, `wiki-ingest`, `wiki-lint`).
